The Fourteenth Summer School on Formal Techniques took place at Menlo College
in May 2025.

Organized by the SRI Formal Methods Program, the school focuses on the
principles and practice of formal techniques, with an emphasis on hands-on use
and development. Lectures and materials are available from the
[[https://ssft-sri.github.io/][Summer School website]].
