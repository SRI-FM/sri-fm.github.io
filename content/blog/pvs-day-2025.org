[[https://shemesh.larc.nasa.gov/nfm2025/pvs-day.html][PVS Day]] is a collaborative workshop dedicated to the Prototype Verification
System (PVS), the formal verification system developed by SRI International
and supported by NASA.

Co-located with [[https://shemesh.larc.nasa.gov/nfm2025/][NASA Formal Methods 2025]], the workshop features expert talks
on current work with PVS, community discussion, and examples of applications
across several domains.
