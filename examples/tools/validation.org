* Semantic sources and scope
- [[https://pvs.csl.sri.com/doc/pvs-language-reference.pdf][PVS Language Reference]]:
  predicate subtypes, function definitions, quantified formulas.
- [[https://pvs.csl.sri.com/documentation.html][PVS documentation]] and
  [[https://pvs.csl.sri.com/introduction.shtml][introduction]]: specification, proof,
  evaluation, and experimental model checking.

  integrated Yices prover commands. Availability of research PVS2C/PVS2Rust
  extensions is qualified according to the site's existing source.
- [[https://yices.csl.sri.com/doc/yices-language.html][Yices input grammar]] and
  [[https://github.com/SRI-CSL/yices2][official source overview]]: native typed
  syntax, APIs, MCSat build requirements.
- [[https://sal.csl.sri.com/documentation.shtml][SAL manuals]], plus local
  sal-3.3/doc/sal_tutorial.tex, sections on the SAL language and model checking:
  contexts, controlled variables, initialization, transitions, and LTL.
- [[https://sal.csl.sri.com/introduction.shtml][SAL suite guide]]: separate checker
  capabilities, limited typechecking, Yices backend, and experimental tools.
